Privacy Policy — Otis

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Otis is a private, single-user app. It is used only with the Google account otis.the.great.26@gmail.com.

What it accesses: that account's Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Sheets and Docs, only to do tasks its owner asks for.

What happens to it: it is kept in that Google account and on the owner's own computer. To do a task, Otis may send the part he needs to the AI services the owner uses to run him. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Google user data: Otis's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other people: Otis collects no data from anyone else.
